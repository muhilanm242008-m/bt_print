Print a PDF or photo on a thermal printer

Four steps. Works with Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) printers that understand ESC/POS, in Chrome or Edge.

1

Connect your printer

Turn the printer on and keep it close. Pick it from the list that opens.

2

Choose a file

A PDF or an image (JPG, PNG, WebP).

3

Adjust the preview

The preview shows exactly what will print, in black and white.

Your preview will appear here.
4

Print

  • Printer connected
  • File ready

Bluetooth only works over HTTPS or localhost. If the device list does not open here, open this page in its own browser tab.
